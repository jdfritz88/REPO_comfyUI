TRAINING SETTINGS — text-encoder training is your choice (c)

The three options were:
    Why there was a choice: no authoritative source gives tested text-encoder values for a real-person SDXL LoRA. OneTrainer's own SDXL LoRA preset has it off; Kohya's SDXL docs recommend training the UNet only. Proceed trains with exactly these settings.

    1 · Videos the search could not call

    Videos that have a face in them the search could not be sure about. Each one is a copy; your own video on the drive is untouched. Nothing has been taken out of these.

    What to do: play a video. If she is in it, pause at the first moment you see her and press She appears here. The video then leaves this list; its copy is kept, and the next search starts that video at the moment you marked instead of reading it from the beginning, and pulls her frames out. If she is not in it, press Not her: the copy is deleted and the decision is written into her search history. Mark it once — Undo at the top takes it back.
    Nothing waiting here.

    2 · Photos the search could not call

    Whole photos, and whole frames pulled out of your videos, where the app could not tell whether the face is hers. Rather than guess, it set them aside for you. Answer one and it leaves the list.

    What to do: answer each picture.
    • Yes, it's her — the picture is kept and cut into crops for training.
    • Yes — but don't use it — it is her, but you do not want it trained on. The decision is written down, the copy is deleted, and nothing about the app's idea of her face changes.
    • Duplicate — the same picture again. Recorded and the copy deleted.
    • Not her — the copy is deleted and no later search will copy that photo again.
    Nothing asks you to confirm. Undo at the top of the page takes back your last answer, and no file is actually deleted while Undo can still reach it.
    Nothing waiting here.

    3 · Face crops

    Her face, cut close out of every photo and frame the search kept. These are what the LoRA learns her face from.

    What to do: look for crops that are wrong — someone else's face still in shot, a sideways picture, a bad cut. Tick those, then use the buttons at the top of the page:
    • Delete ticked — the crop goes to the Recycle Bin, and the app writes down that the photo it came from is not her, so no later search copies it again.
    • Rotate Left / Rotate Right — turns a sideways crop a quarter turn and measures her face again.
    Anything you leave alone is kept. A blue outline means you have ticked it.
    Yellow frame: someone else is still in the picture. When a crop is cut, the app finds every face in it, works out which is hers, and shrinks the edges to push the others out without ever cutting into her face. Yellow means that failed — the other face overlaps hers, or trimming far enough would have left too little picture. Three ways to deal with one:
    • Blur other faces — the other faces are blurred away where they stand and the crop is kept. Blur, not a black box, because a hard black rectangle is a shape the training would learn.
    • Yes — but don't use it — it is her, and you don't want it trained on. The crop goes to the Recycle Bin and the decision is recorded. Nothing is learned from it, and the photo is not marked as somebody else.
    • Delete ticked — for a crop that isn't her at all. The crop goes to the Recycle Bin and the app records that the photo it came from is not this person, so no later search copies it again.
    Find near duplicates looks for crops showing her at the same moment — it finds her face, straightens it, and compares her head angle and the look of her face, eyes and mouth. The background, her clothes and how the picture was framed do not count. The groups are shown one under another, divided by a green line. Every picture in a group is ticked except the one with the biggest, sharpest view of her face, which is left unticked and marked BEST. Agree by pressing that group's own Yes — but don't use it or Delete ticked, up beside its heading, or change the ticks first. Those buttons touch that group and nothing else; the ones in the toolbar stand down while a scan is showing, because the ticks belong to the whole page. The slider decides how alike counts as the same; move it and scan again. A group you have dealt with, or pressed Keep all of these on, leaves the queue.
    Leaving one alone trains on it, other face and all. Filter: yellow framed only at the top hides everything else, so you can work through just these; while it is on, Tick all ticks only what is shown.
    4 left = only a face that has barely moved · right = looser, so a small turn or a change of expression still groups. Scanning only ticks; nothing is deleted.
    No face crops.

    4 · Body crops

    A wider cut of the same pictures, head to somewhere below the waist, so the LoRA also learns her build and how she stands rather than a face floating on its own.

    What to do: the same as face crops — tick the bad ones and use Delete ticked or the rotate buttons at the top of the page. Anything you leave alone is kept.
    4 left = only a face that has barely moved · right = looser, so a small turn or a change of expression still groups. Scanning only ticks; nothing is deleted.
    No body crops.

    When you are finished, Proceed to training at the top uses every crop still left in sections 3 and 4 — whatever you ticked or did not tick — and starts the run. If anything you answered "Yes, it's her" or marked "She appears here" has not been turned into crops yet, it stops and offers to do that first, so nothing you approved is left out.
